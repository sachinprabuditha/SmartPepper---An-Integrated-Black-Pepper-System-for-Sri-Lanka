Overview  This document provides structured knowledge on all currently recommended black pepper (Piper nigrum) varieties for cultivatio n in Sri  Lanka. It covers two internationally introduced commercial varieties, one local DEA - recognised selection, and three newly   released DEA  hybrid varieties   —   totalling six active varieties.  2. Panniyur - 1 and Kuching were introduced to Sri Lanka in the 1970s and remain the most widely planted commercial varieties.  3.1 Panniyur - 1  Origin   Introduced   —   India (1970s)  Specialities   World's first pepper hybrid; long spikes and very high yield. Parent  of all three DEA hybrid varieties.  Suitability   Vigorous grower that thrives in open areas with high humidity.  Best for low to mid - country wet zones.  Soil Recommendation   Prefers well - drained loamy soil.  Planting Spacing   2.5 x 2.5 meters  Vine Density   1600 vines per hectare  Pit Dimensions   60 x 60 x 60 cm  3.2 Kuching  Origin   Introduced   —   Malaysia (1970s)  Specialities   High piperine content and excellent dry - to - green recovery ratio.  Most adaptable to adverse conditions.  Suitability   Tolerates drier zones and adverse soil conditions. Recommended  across wet, intermediate, and dry zones.  Soil Recommendation   Thrives in sandy loam or well - drained gravelly soil.  Planting Spacing   2.0 x 2.0 meters  Vine Density   2500 vines per hectare  Pit Dimensions   50 x 50 x 50 cm

4. Local Selection   —   GK 49  GK 49 is the Department of Export Agriculture's officially recognised premier local selection of black pepper in Sri Lanka. I t is a high - yielding,  superior - quality variety that replaces the previously used generic 'Local' classification. GK 49 is widely gr own by pepper cultivators across  wet and intermediate zones.  4.1 GK 49  Origin   Local Selection   —   Sri Lanka (DEA)  Specialities   Sri Lanka's premier high - yielding local selection with superior  pungency and quality. Replaces the generic 'Local' variety  classification.  Suitability   Highly adaptable to Sri Lankan agro - climatic conditions. Performs  best in Red - Yellow Podzolic soils of wet and intermediate zones.  Soil Recommendation   Adapts well to Red - Yellow Podzolic, loamy, and clay - loam soils.  Planting Spacing   2.5 x 2.5 meters  Vine Density   1600 vines per hectare  Pit Dimensions   60 x 60 x 60 cm  5.
