Soil Type Reference and Implications Five primary soil types are found across Sri Lanka's black pepper cultivation zones. Understanding each soil's characteristics helps determine appropriate variety selection, fertilizer management, and irrigation strategy. Soil Type Description & Implications for Black Pepper Loamy Balanced mix of sand, silt, and clay. Best water retention and drainage for pepper. Found across all zones. Clay Loam Higher clay content. Good nutrient holding capacity. Common in mid and up - country elevations. Requires drainage management. Red - Yellow Podzolic Highly weathered tropical soils. Naturally acidic. Widespread in wet and intermediate zones. Requires lime/dolomite to correct pH. Gravelly Coarse - textured with free drainage. Common in dry - intermediate zones. Low moisture retention — mulching is essential. Sandy High permeability, low nutrient and water retention. Found in coastal and dry zone districts. Heavy organic matter inputs required. Key Insight: Red - Yellow Podzolic soils are the most widespread in Sri Lanka's established black pepper regions (Central, Sabaragamuwa, Uva provinces). These soils require pH correction with Dolomite (500 g/plant every 2 years) as described in the Maintenance knowledge domain. 4. DEA Recommended Hybrid Varieties The Department of Export Agriculture (DEA) of Sri Lanka has recently introduced three new hybrid black pepper varieties — Dingi Rala, Bootawe Rala, and Kohukumbure Rala. These hybrids were developed from crosses of high - performing parents and are now avail able for commercial cultivation. Note: IW 5 is no longer an active variety in Sri Lanka and has been replaced by these DEA hybrids in district recommendations throughout this document. Important Update: IW 5 has been discontinued as a recommended variety in Sri Lanka. Farmers previously growing or planning to plant IW 5 should switch to the appropriate DEA hybrid based on their district. Additionally, GK 49 is recognised as Sri Lanka's superior local sele ction (not a separate 'Local' variety) — all district recommendations now correctly reflect GK 49 in this role.
