This allows the support to establish roots and provide adequate shade before pepper planting. • Ongoing Management: Lop the Gliricidia canopy twice per year to prevent overshading. Lopped leaves and branches make excellent green mulch at the base of the vine. Why Gliricidia? Gliricidia sepium is a nitrogen - fixing legume, meaning it improves soil fertility naturally. Its fast growth, easy propagation from cuttings, and tolerance to lopping make it ideal as a long - term pepper support in Sri Lanka's agro - climatic conditions. 4. Field Planting Field planting must be timed, spaced, and executed correctly to ensure strong vine establishment. Poor planting technique at this stage is difficult to correct later. 4.1 Field Planting Quick Reference All critical field planting specifications are summarised below: Parameter Specification / Guideline Recommended Spacing 2.4 m × 2.4 m (mono - crop and intercrop with coconut) Planting Density 1,700 plants per hectare Pit Dimensions 60 cm × 60 cm × 60 cm Pit Fill Mix Topsoil + cow dung or compost (well mixed) Support Stick Dimensions 3 – 5 cm diameter, 2.5 m length (Gliricidia) Support Planting Depth 20 cm into the ground at the corner of the planting pit Support Lead Time At least 6 months before pepper planting Plant Age at Field Planting 4 – 6 months old nursery plants Plant Condition 5 – 8 healthy leaves, vigorous growth Distance from Support 15 – 20 cm away from the support stick Planting Season Onset of monsoon rains Post - Planting Actions Provide temporary shade; apply mulch to base to conserve soil moisture 4.2 Land Preparation and Pit Filling • Land Preparation: Prepare the field by clearing weeds, breaking up compacted soil, and improving drainage where necessary. • Pit Dimensions: Dig planting pits of 60 cm × 60 cm × 60 cm at the marked spacing of 2.4 m × 2.4 m. • Pit Fill: Fill pits with a mixture of topsoil and well - composted cow dung or compost to a level that allows the plant to sit at the correct depth. • Settling Time: Allow filled pits to settle for at least 2 weeks before planting. 4.3 Planting Procedure 4. Plant at the onset of monsoon rains — this is the single most critical timing factor for successful establishment. 5.
