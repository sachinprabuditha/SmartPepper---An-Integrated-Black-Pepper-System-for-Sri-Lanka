Industry Challenges

Sri Lanka's pepper industry faces several critical challenges that affect sustainability and global competitiveness.

Biosafety Risks

Diseases like Leaf Fall or Foot Rot often go undetected until catastrophic crop loss occurs, with a severe lack of early warning systems.

Subjective Grading

Manual quality assessment leads to disputes and unfair compensation. Grading outcomes are inconsistent due to human judgment.

Economic Volatility

Farmers lack access to real-time market prices, resulting in high-quality yields often being sold at suboptimal rates to intermediaries.

Supply Chain Opacity

There is no standardized digital record for traceability, making it difficult to meet modern sustainability and global transparency standards.

The Integrated Solution

SmartPepper is comprised of four powerful, inter-connected modules built using cutting-edge technologies.

01

Disease Detection & Analysis

Lahiru J W A N • AI/Mobile

An all-in-one AI mobile app that provides automated, location-aware disease detection, severity estimation, remedy recommendations, and disease spread forecasting.

  • Multi-Class ML Detection
  • Severity Prediction
  • Geolocation Mapping
  • Spread Forecasting
02

IoT Automated Grading

Premasiri J G P L • IoT/Hardware

A low-cost, real-time grading system combining physical density measurement (Gram-Liter) and computer vision assessment to meet international export standards.

  • Load-cell Density Test
  • Conveyor Automation
  • ML Visual Classification
  • Web Control Interface
03

Price & Plantation Management

Liyanage S K R • ML/NLP

An intelligent system offering price predictions, yield valuation, seasonal data analytics, and a RAG-based AI chat assistant for agronomy guidance.

  • Market Price Forecasting
  • RAG AI Chat Assistant
  • Bluetooth Scale Integration
  • Seasonal Trend Analytics
04

Blockchain Auction System

Prabuditha K. S • Web3/Fullstack

A decentralized, transparent auction platform using Ethereum smart contracts, NFT-based digital passports, and IPFS for immutable supply chain records.

  • Smart Contract Escrow
  • Real-time Live Bidding
  • NFT Digital Passports
  • IPFS Document Storage
